Karahi Cut

Small bone-in pieces that cook fast and soak up masala.

Best cooking method: Cook on high heat in a wok (karahi): fry in own fat for 25-30 minutes.

Order this cut

Biryani Cut

Medium bone-in pieces that stay juicy in long, slow cooking.

Best cooking method: Layer with rice and cook on dum (slow steam) for 45-60 minutes.

Order this cut

Boneless Mince

Trimmed lean meat and fine mince, no bones or excess fat.

Best cooking method: Shape into kebabs or shami; pan-fry or grill; or cook as keema.
