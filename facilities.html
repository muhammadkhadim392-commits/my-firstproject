Deboning Hall

Our deboning hall is equipped with modern tools and skilled professionals to ensure precise and efficient deboning of meat. We maintain strict hygiene and quality control at every stage to deliver premium cuts.

Cold Storage

Our cold storage facilities maintain the perfect temperature to keep meat fresh and safe. We use advanced refrigeration systems to ensure consistent quality and longer shelf life.

  • Temperature controlled
  • Freshness preserved
  • Safe and reliable storage

Our blast freezer quickly reduces the temperature of meat, locking in freshness, flavor, and nutrients. This process helps maintain the highest quality and extends shelf life.

  • Quick freezing process
  • Maintains quality and taste
  • Longer shelf life

Packaging Hall

  • Hygienic packaging process

  • Safe and durable packing
